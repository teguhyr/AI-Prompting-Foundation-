**Principles of AI Reasoning**

- Understand the basics of AI reasoning and how it processes information sequentially.
- Study how **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)** improves AI decision-making by breaking tasks into logical steps.
- Practice simple CoT prompts for tasks like summarization or data extraction.
- Learn to apply CoT to complex workflows like analyzing customer data, generating reports, or solving business problems.

**Improving AI Reasoning Capabilities**

- Explore **Tree-of-Thought (ToT)** prompting, where AI explores multiple paths before selecting the best answer.
- Study how ToT can provide multiple solutions to a single problem, offering various approaches.
- Test CoT and ToT techniques in creative tasks, such as generating marketing campaigns or business strategies.

**Utilize AI to Solve Complex Problems**

- Implement CoT and ToT prompting in advanced scenarios like customer journey mapping, product development, or content ideation.
- Learn to combine CoT and ToT prompts for multi-layered workflows, where AI assists with both planning and execution.
- Explore the potential for using ToT in strategic foresight and long-term planning for business growth and innovation.
