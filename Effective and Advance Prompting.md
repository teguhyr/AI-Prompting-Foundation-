**Understanding The Fundamentals of Prompts in AI**

- Learn about the essence of prompt engineering
- Understand how AI process language input & its correlation to prompt structuring
- Study basic prompting techniques like zero-shot and few-shot learning.

**Deep Dive into More Specific Prompt**

- Learn how to frame more complex prompts to get precise and relevant outputs.
- Explore how to craft prompts that direct AI to follow certain thinking processes.
- Explore industry-specific applications of prompt engineering (e.g., for marketing, customer service).

**Introduction to Advance Prompting**

- Understand Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting for sequential reasoning and improved logical responses.
- Study Tree-of-Thought (ToT) prompting to generate multiple outcomes or solutions for a given problem.
- Explore meta-prompting, where AI generates prompts based on initial user input for more tailored results such as strategic marketing, operational optimization, or creative problem-solving.
