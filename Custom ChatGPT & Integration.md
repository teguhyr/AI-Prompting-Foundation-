**Get Familiar with GPTs**

- Understand how language models like ChatGPT generate text and why they’re effective.
- Explore how to use ChatGPT in different contexts, from drafting emails to generating reports.
- Recognize the limitations of default models and why customization is necessary for specific business needs.

**Learn to Customize GPTs**

- Learn how to customize ChatGPT prompts to improve accuracy in specific domains (e.g., industry-specific language).
- Explore techniques for adjusting tone, style, and format based on the task or audience.
- Practice building workflows where ChatGPT handles multiple steps in a process (e.g., customer queries, document generation).

**Train the GPTs**

- Build and train **CustomGPT** models tailored to your business by feeding them domain-specific datasets.
- Master the integration of CustomGPT with APIs to automate tasks like customer support, marketing emails, or content curation.
- Dive into advanced NLP (Natural Language Processing) techniques like entity recognition and sentiment analysis to further refine responses.
- Implement complex AI models that support business operations across various departments, such as HR, marketing, and sales.
